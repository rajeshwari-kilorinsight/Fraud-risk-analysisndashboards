# Fraud-risk-analysisndashboards
I recently worked on a Power BI-based Customer Credit & Risk Analytics project, where I transformed a large customer dataset into an interactive analytical report focused on credit behaviour, payment patterns, and risk indicators.

🔹 Customer Analysis – explored customer profiles, income, occupation and financial characteristics.
🔹 Credit Analysis – analyzed credit scores, credit mix, credit utilization, outstanding debt, loans and credit inquiries.
🔹 Payment Analysis – evaluated payment behaviour, delayed payments, payment delays, EMI and monthly balances.
🔹 Risk Analysis – examined key risk indicators such as poor credit, high utilization, payment delays and debt exposure.
🔹 Risk Monitoring – tracked changes in credit and payment indicators over time using monthly trends.

The project helped me strengthen my skills in Power BI, data visualization, KPI development, DAX, financial analysis and risk-oriented business intelligence.

Key learning: A good dashboard is not just about presenting numbers—it is about converting data into insights that can support better business decisions.
